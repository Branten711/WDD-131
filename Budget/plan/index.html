Overview

Purpose

The purpose is to make an entertaining budgeting game that is also informational about life

Audience

Anyone who wants to play a budgeting game

Dynamic elements

* The tacker of current money balance

* an array of random expenses

* a level system where you get the opportunity to make more money (depending on what I get done maybe adding the opportunity to hire employees)

* different functions to subtract from the money balance or add.

* being able to earn money by going to work.
